Mohammed Pari - "Mohammeds page" (converted from Figma)

HOW TO VIEW
  Double-click index.html. It opens in your default browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  Keep the folder structure as-is: index.html needs the css/ and images/ folders next to it.
  At 1183px wide or more, every card sits at its exact Figma position (1183 x 1232 canvas),
  and the whole design scales up to fill wider windows (no empty side bars).
  Narrower windows (tablets/phones) stack the cards in a single column.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html        page structure and text
  css/style.css     all styling (colors, fonts, spacing, card positions)
  images/           photos, portraits and the image-based sections

EDITING
  Card positions are set on each card in index.html, e.g. style="--x:19px;--y:403px;--w:270px"
  (x = left, y = top, w = width, same numbers as in Figma). Edit, save, refresh the browser.
  Interests & Passions, My Journey, My Team Connections, Quick Highlights, the quote card and
  the "Same team" strip are pictures in the Figma file, so they are high-resolution (2x) images
  here (images/). The header, About Me, My Identity, Unique Insight and Destinations are real text.
